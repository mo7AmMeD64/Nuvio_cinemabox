# Nuvio 1.8.0 — Draft

Local draft; release content and validation are not final.

## Added

- Glass UI brings panels into floating islands, with glass or solid materials and appearance controls.
- Settings v2 adds category navigation, live previews and usage guidance.
- Player controls, loading states and prompts use the island design.
- Details, library, search, schedule and social views receive refreshed layouts.
- Social adds clearer refresh and private/loading states, with corrected green presence indicators.

## Fixed

- Apple TV detail pages keep the reading veil and focus-only action fan, with expanded text and a locked carousel.
- Episode cards show durations.
- Menu, settings and player focus states receive visual and interaction fixes.

Social service identity across Trakt, Simkl and Letterboxd is not fully unified, statistics are incomplete, and the broader Social migration remains in progress. No deployment or physical LG/Samsung validation is claimed for this draft.
