# Home loading on Android TV

Read-only Android logs on 2026-10-03 showed the disabled Minha TV manifest timing out twice, at 13:43:36.944 and 13:43:56.946, approximately 20 seconds per attempt. Once declarations were assembled, the six requested catalogues completed between 13:43:57.436 and 13:43:57.770, with all seven Home rows assembled at 13:43:57.812. No endpoint, credential or private account response is included here.

The consumer treated a completed failed prefetch as a cache miss and requested the same manifest again. It also waited for disabled add-ons before assembling active catalogues. Enabled manifests now receive worker priority, disabled pending manifests are not awaited by Home, and failed completed attempts are not immediately retried. Workers skip cached slots. All-cache prefetch retains ready slots; workers also start when the first slots are cached but later slots need fetching. Catalogue/declaration order remains unchanged. The prefetch and cache remain bounded at 12 entries; larger active lists still have an inline fallback.

The clock island exposes real Home activity and an expandable panel (blue key/CH+ or pointer click) showing stage, elapsed seconds, active prefetched add-ons checked, rows refreshed and failures. It has a Close button and does not cancel or restart loading. Counters cover the current assembly pass; they are not a fabricated percentage or an estimate of remaining time. Home remains usable underneath. Counters for the bounded manifest prefetch do not claim coverage of inline fallback requests.

Apple TV layout polish adds space at the panel edge and below the profile header, improves Streaming label contrast, and uses a neutral translucent surface with a restrained top reflection. The carousel gradient now covers the text area and bottom of the card. Existing agenda information appears above the logo as an accent badge, without inventing release dates.

Validation: production manifest consumer/worker fixture covers disabled pending, completed failure, cache skip, all-cache reuse and active-first priority; normal and ASan/UBSan runs pass. Shared manifest cache test, translation alignment for all 28 languages, full local build and detail/navigation regression pass.
